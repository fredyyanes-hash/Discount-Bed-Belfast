Our story

A local bed shop, run by people who know sleep

Since 2000, Discount Beds Belfast has helped families across Northern Ireland find the right bed at the right price. Our Templemore Avenue showroom is full of beds, mattresses and bedroom furniture you can see, touch and try before you buy.

From divan and wooden beds to memory foam and pocket-sprung mattresses, children's beds and bunk beds, our range covers every room and every budget. We deliver and assemble throughout Northern Ireland, and our friendly team is always on hand to help.
